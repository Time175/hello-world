# Used Converted Van Search

**Search date:** May 6, 2026

## Criteria

| Requirement | Target |
|---|---|
| Price | Under $60,000 |
| Chassis | AWD Sprinter preferred; other chassis acceptable |
| Mileage | Under 100,000 |
| Required gear | Solar, batteries, refrigerator, heater, cooling (A/C), toilet |
| Nice to have | Outdoor shower |

## Headline finding

**The "AWD Sprinter under $60k with a full build" essentially does not exist on the open market right now.** Even un‑converted 2024 AWD Sprinter chassis are selling at ~$60k by themselves, and professionally converted AWD/4x4 Sprinters are clustering at **$95k–$165k**. To stay under $60k you almost certainly have to drop either the AWD Sprinter chassis or the "professionally built" expectation (or both).

That said, there are several legitimate matches on **non‑AWD Sprinter / Transit / Promaster** chassis, plus one borderline AWD Transit listing that hits your budget exactly.

## Practical recommendation

Given the data, the realistic paths under $60k with all six required systems are:

1. **2021 Ram Promaster 2WD, Ansonia CT, $48k, 85.6k mi** — strongest on‑paper match: best price, biggest battery (600Ah), real solar, real heater, real toilet. Verify A/C in person.
2. **2020 Sprinter 2WD, Spokane WA, ~$60k, 53k mi** — best Sprinter‑family match. You give up AWD but get a low‑mileage Sprinter with A/C and an installed shower.
3. **2021 Ford Transit AWD, Santa Barbara CA, $60k, 23.5k mi** — only AWD listing actually inside budget. You'd need to budget extra for finishing the plumbing/trim and confirming it's still available.

If staying with an **AWD Sprinter** is non‑negotiable, the data says the realistic floor is ~$95k for a complete used build, or ~$60k for a bare AWD chassis that you (or a builder) would convert.
